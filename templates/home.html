When facts change

Living fact ledger

Every price, number and claim has an owner, a source and an expiry. Change one and Canon finds the published content that quietly went out of date.

When rules don't work

Guideline debugger

If reviewers keep overriding a rule, the rule is probably unclear. Canon shows the reasons people gave and helps the brand team rewrite it.

When a fix is needed

Intent-first fixes

Canon says what the writer meant, then changes only the flagged words. Nothing is applied until someone clicks.
