What Canon does

One gate between a draft and your customers

Text, PDFs and images go through the same rules. Every flag cites its source, every fix is minimal, and every decision is logged.

✎
Check

Rules, facts and voice

Banned claims, preferred terms, required disclaimers and wrong numbers are caught by exact rules. Tone and unsupported claims are reviewed by AI, and each AI finding is validated.

◩
See

Images and accessibility

Canon reads the text inside images and runs it through the same checks, measures WCAG contrast, catches retired logos, and makes sure there's alt text.

⇶
Route

Risk-based review

Low-risk pieces take a fast lane. Anything that makes financial claims goes to Legal. Every routing decision lists its reasons.

↻
Keep current

Living fact ledger

Change a price and Canon finds every approved piece that still uses the old one, drafts the update, and sends it back to review.

✓
Prove

Signed approval seals

Every approval is signed. Anyone can paste text, upload an image or enter a seal code to see if it's approved, outdated, or modified, with the exact changes.

⌂
Partner

Agency workspace

Agencies get briefs, a brand kit, live checks and reviewer feedback in one place, and never see internal facts.
