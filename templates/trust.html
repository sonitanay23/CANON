How Canon uses AI

Canon advises; people decide. Here is exactly what the AI does, what it sees, and what keeps it honest.

Checked without AI

Banned words, preferred terms, required disclaimers, punctuation rules, and numbers that don't match the fact ledger. These are exact, repeatable checks with no AI involved.

Checked with AI

Tone, unsupported or exaggerated claims, what the writer meant, and the wording of a suggested fix. Every AI finding is labelled and shows a confidence score.

What keeps the AI honest

  • Every finding must quote the draft exactly and cite a real rule or fact ID. Anything else is dropped before you see it, and the number dropped is shown.
  • If the rule engine already found an issue, its exact finding wins over the AI's.
  • Low-confidence and high-severity findings are marked for human review.
  • Nothing is applied, approved or published without a person clicking.
  • If the AI is down or returns bad output twice, Canon still shows rule-engine results and says the AI review was unavailable.
  • The reliability scorecard measures misses and false flags on a fixed test set.
