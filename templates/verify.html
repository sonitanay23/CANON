How it works

  1. When a reviewer approves a piece, Canon signs it with HMAC-SHA256 over the exact text, the image file and the version.
  2. Whitespace and quote styles are ignored. Any other change, even one digit in a price, breaks the match.
  3. Images are matched by file hash, then by visual fingerprint; near matches have their text read and compared.
  4. If a fact changes and the piece is updated, its old seal shows as outdated.
